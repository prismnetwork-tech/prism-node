# Governance

Prism Network maintainers steward the node daemon through public issues,
design proposals and reviewed pull requests.

Privilege, isolation and protocol changes require:

- A written compatibility and migration analysis.
- Tests covering old and new behavior.
- Review from two maintainers when the maintainer group has at least two
  active members.
- Hardware-canary evidence before production adoption.

Security fixes may be prepared privately and disclosed after supported
consumers can update.
